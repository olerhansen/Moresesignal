Privacy Policy
Effective Date: August 2026
Morse Signal respects your privacy.

Information Collection:
Morse Signal does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
The app does not require user accounts and does not track your activity.

Camera / Flashlight:
Morse Signal uses your device's flashlight solely to transmit Morse code signals.
The app does not capture, record, or store photos or videos.

Data Sharing:
No personal information is shared with third parties.

Analytics:
Morse Signal does not use analytics or advertising services.
